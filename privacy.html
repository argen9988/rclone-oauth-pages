Privacy Policy — rclone-second-brain

Last updated: 2026-10-09

rclone-second-brain is a Google OAuth client for personal use by its owner only. It is used by rclone, running on the owner's own computer, to read and write the owner's own Google Drive.

What data is accessed

Only files in the owner's own Google Drive, through the Google Drive API scopes the owner grants when signing in.

How it is used

Files are copied between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, person, or service.

Sharing, selling, and AI training

No data is shared with or sold to anyone. No Google user data is used to develop, improve, or train AI or machine learning models.

Storage

Synced files and the sign-in credentials stay on the owner's own computer. The developer runs no server and keeps no copies elsewhere.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

隱私權政策：這個用戶端只供擁有者本人使用，只存取擁有者自己的 Google 雲端硬碟，檔案只在擁有者的電腦與雲端硬碟之間同步；不分享、不販售、不用來訓練 AI；沒有伺服器，不在別處保存任何資料。可隨時在 Google 帳戶的「第三方連線」撤銷存取權。
