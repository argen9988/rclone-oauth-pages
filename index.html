rclone-second-brain

rclone-second-brain is a personal Google OAuth client used by one person, the owner of this page, to let rclone sync the owner's own Google Drive files with the owner's own computer.

It is not a public service. No one else is invited to sign in, and it does not collect data from anyone.

See the privacy policy.
